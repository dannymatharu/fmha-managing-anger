Part 1

Audit the Four Pillars

Evidence beats instinct. “We usually do this” is not evidence. A named document, briefing, message or record is.

1

Clear Expectations

Can every player, parent, coach and volunteer describe the standard before there is a problem?

2

Visible Leadership

Do leaders model the standard when emotions are high, including with friends and influential adults?

3

Consistent Consequences

Would the same behaviour receive the same response, regardless of status, role or match result?

4

Proactive Communication

Does the club discuss behaviour before fixtures and pressure points, not only after an incident?
